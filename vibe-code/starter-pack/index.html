Three sections, eight working examples, and nothing else.

Every page is a single self-contained HTML file — open it and it works. No install, no accounts, no internet needed. Replace the examples with your own, or delete them and start from the empty folders. This page has no JavaScript at all: adding a tool means pasting four lines into index.html.

01

Student Quiz

3 items

Self-marking practice a student does on a device.

  • Adding and Subtracting Integers Stage 4 · Number · MA4-INT-C-01 Ten questions, two goes at each, covering all four sign cases. Names the mistake and shows the jump on a number line.
  • Fraction to Percentage Stage 4 · Number · MA4-FRC-C-01 Four levels, Sweet to Spicy. Estimate on the line first, then convert; one part is named for you if you want it.
  • Reading a Dot Plot Stage 4 · Statistics · PLACEHOLDER-OUTCOME Mean, median, mode and range off a dot plot. The four measures are each other's misconceptions, so a wrong answer gets named. Its teacher panel can self-test the marker.

Your next quiz: make a folder under student-quizzes/, then copy the <li> above and point it at your new file.

02

Interactive Tools

3 items

Things you drive on the board in front of a class.

  • Fraction Bar Stage 4 · Number · MA4-FRC-C-01 Drag a fraction across a bar and a number line at once. Pin one to find equivalents; decimals shown exactly, recurring dots and all.
  • Fraction to Percentage Stage 4 · Number · MA4-FRC-C-01 One number line read from both sides. Step through it with the class: partition the whole, name one part, count on.
  • Angles on a Line and at a Point Stage 4 · Measurement and Space · PLACEHOLDER-OUTCOME Drag a ray and every angle changes while the total does not. Hide one, call it x, and the tool becomes the question.

Your next tool: one folder per tool under interactive-tools/, each with its own index.html.

03

Worksheet Creator

2 items

Generates a printable page, with answers.

  • Times Tables Practice Stage 3 · Number · MA3-MR-01 Choose the tables and the length, then print up to three versions of the same sheet — same questions, different order.
  • Fraction to Percentage Stage 4 · Number · MA4-FRC-C-01 Prints the same double number line the class was taught with — the whole partitioned, the fraction marked, a blank under the tick.

Your next creator: every one should print cleanly at A4. See the print CSS at the bottom of the example.
