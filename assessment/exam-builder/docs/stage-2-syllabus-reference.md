# Stage 2 — syllabus reference for the question banks

NESA Mathematics K–10 (2022), **Stage 2 (Years 3–4)**. The 20 outcome codes and
statements below come from the NESA outcomes page (checked 2026-09-25). The
content under each focus area is a **paraphrase for planning**. The official
Stage 2 content document was not available in this build, so check the A/B
split against it when it is. Every prompt in the banks is original.

## Design principles for Stage 2 (read before adding a type)

1. **The picture carries the maths.** Most types show a model: base-ten
   blocks, a place-value chart, a number line, an array, equal groups, a
   fraction strip, a clock, a scale or a jug. The student reads, counts or
   completes the model. That is the skill being built.
2. **Low reading load.**
   - Prompts are one or two short sentences, with no more than about 15 words
     in a sentence where possible.
   - Use everyday words, and write numbers as numerals.
   - Put the question word first ("How many…", "Which…", "Write…").
   - Context sentences are optional. The maths never depends on reading a
     paragraph.
3. **Big ideas, not tricks.** Each bank is built around the big ideas of its
   focus area:
   - place value as grouping in tens, and trading;
   - part–part–whole and the inverse link between + and −;
   - multiplication as equal groups and arrays, and division as sharing or
     grouping;
   - fractions as equal parts, and as lengths on a number line;
   - measuring as repeating a unit;
   - angle as an amount of turn;
   - a graph as data organised to answer a question.
4. **Skill building first, assessment-ready too.** Types progress within a
   bank from recognise, to represent, to apply. Every type still has an
   answer, working and marks, so a teacher can build a test from it. Empty
   boxes (a `null` label) show where to write.
5. **Engines.**
   - `manipulatives-engine` is the Stage 2 engine: base-ten blocks, place-value
     charts, scaled number lines, tenths and hundredths grids, equal groups,
     arrays, shape cards, compass rose, angle cards with a right-angle tester,
     tally marks, fraction strips, fraction shapes, pan balance.
   - Reused engines: `measure` (clocks, scales, jugs, time lines), `grid`
     (grid maps, symmetry, slides, flips and turns), `solids` (3D objects and
     nets), `statistics` (tables, dot plots, column graphs, pictograms),
     `probability` (spinners, bags), `open-number-line` (jump strategies),
     `bar-model` (part–whole, compare), `length` (ruler), `area`
     (grid-count), `volume` (cube arrays).

## The teaching sequence (the order of the Stage 2 topic list)

Part A (typically Year 3) then Part B (typically Year 4). Within each part the
order is number first, because every later topic leans on it: place value →
addition and subtraction → multiplication and division → fractions. Then
measurement and space, then data and chance.

## Content, by focus area (paraphrased)

**Place value A.**
- Read, write, represent and order numbers to 10 000 with blocks, charts, words and numerals.
- Partition standard (3 thousands, 4 hundreds…) and non-standard (34 hundreds…).
- Zero as a placeholder.
- Count by 10s, 100s and 1000s from any number.
- 10 more and 10 less; 100 more and 100 less.

**Place value B.**
- Numbers to tens of thousands; expanded form.
- Rounding to the nearest 10, 100 and 1000.
- Tenths and hundredths as parts of a whole (grids), and decimal notation.
- Decimals on a number line.
- Comparing and ordering decimals to 2 decimal places.
- Money as dollars and cents.

**Additive relations A.**
- 2-digit ± 2-digit by jump, split, bridging 10 and near doubles.
- Related facts and fact families (the inverse).
- Missing-number sentences.
- Part–part–whole word problems with a bar model.
- Estimating first.

**Additive relations B.**
- 3-digit numbers: mental strategies, the column (written) method with trading, and estimating with rounding.
- Checking with the inverse.
- Money, change and two-step problems; missing numbers.

**Multiplicative relations A.**
- Equal groups, arrays and repeated addition.
- The ×2, ×3, ×4, ×5 and ×10 facts; doubling (×2 → ×4 → ×8).
- Division as sharing and as grouping; linking × and ÷.
- Commutative arrays; odd and even; missing numbers.

**Multiplicative relations B.**
- All facts to 10 × 10, including ×6, ×7, ×8 and ×9 via known facts.
- Splitting arrays (distributive property); 2-digit × 1-digit.
- Division facts and remainders; multiples.
- Missing numbers and word problems.

**Partitioned fractions A.**
- Equal parts.
- Halves → quarters → eighths by halving; thirds → sixths.
- Fractions of a length or shape; naming the shaded fraction.
- Unit fractions on a number line from 0 to 1.
- Comparing unit fractions.

**Partitioned fractions B.**
- Fifths → tenths.
- Number lines beyond 1; counting by fractions.
- Equivalent fractions (1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8, …).
- Comparing and ordering fractions with the same numerator or denominator.

**Geometric measure A.**
- Grid maps (letter–number references) and routes with left/right/forward and quarter turns.
- Measuring in m and cm with a ruler; estimating; comparing lengths.
- Angles as turns and in shapes; comparing to a right angle.

**Geometric measure B.**
- Compass directions (4 and 8 points).
- Millimetres; converting between m, cm and mm; perimeter.
- Classifying angles as acute, right, obtuse, straight or reflex.

**2D spatial A.**
- Sides, vertices and right angles; parallel sides.
- Polygons (triangle to octagon); quadrilaterals by name; regular and irregular.
- Area by counting square units.

**2D spatial B.**
- Combining and splitting shapes.
- Slide, flip and turn on a grid; line symmetry.
- Area in cm² and m²; comparing and estimating areas.

**3D spatial A.**
- Faces, edges and vertices.
- Prisms and pyramids named by their base.
- Everyday objects; nets of cubes, prisms and pyramids.

**3D spatial B.**
- Sketching objects; matching views.
- Capacity in L and mL, read from jugs; 1 L = 1000 mL.
- Volume in cm³ by counting cubes.

**Non-spatial measure A.**
- kg and g on scales; heavier and lighter on a balance.
- Time to 5 minutes and to the minute, analog and digital; quarter past and quarter to.

**Non-spatial measure B.**
- 1 kg = 1000 g; ordering masses; estimating.
- Seconds; am and pm; durations and time lines; converting hours and minutes.

**Data A.**
- Collecting with tallies; tables.
- Dot plots; one-to-one column graphs and pictograms; reading totals and differences.

**Data B.**
- Many-to-one scales and keys; constructing graphs on a given scale.
- Comparing categories and drawing conclusions.

**Chance A.**
- Chance language (certain, likely, unlikely, impossible, even chance).
- Listing the outcomes of spinners, dice and coins.

**Chance B.**
- Conducting and recording experiments.
- Expected versus actual results; fairness; comparing the chances of events.

### Engines added or extended for Stage 2

- **`engines/manipulatives/manipulatives-engine.js`** (new, `MMT_MANIPULATIVES_ENGINE`, base text units 13). It draws:
  - base-ten blocks, number and digit cards, place-value charts;
  - scaled number lines with hops, ten-frames and hundred grids;
  - equal groups, arrays with a split line, 2D shape cards, compass roses;
  - angle cards with an optional dashed right-angle tester (`plain: true` drops the square marker);
  - tally marks and **tally charts** (`rows`), fraction walls and fraction shapes;
  - a pan balance with labelled blocks;
  - a **column-sum** place-value grid for the written method, with trade boxes;
  - a **ruler** with cm/mm marks and an object that need not start at 0.
- **`grid-engine`** gains:
  - `north: true`, a north arrow;
  - `path`, a red route with an arrowhead;
  - `countable: true`, which redraws grid lines over the shapes so the squares can be counted;
  - `shapes[].labelAt`.
- **`measure-engine`**:
  - clock numbers are drawn over the hands with a white halo;
  - the scale takes `labelSize`, and its labels are drawn over the needle.
- **`probability-engine`**: the chance scale takes `numbers: false`, `wordList` and `wordSize`, which gives the Stage 2 chance line (Impossible → Certain, with no fractions).
- The Stage 3 banks now export helpers that Stage 2 reuses:
  - `makeMove` from `two-d-space-area/extra-types.js`;
  - `NETS` and `sq` from `three-d-space-volume`.

### Testing

`node tools/stage2.mjs` generates 80 questions for each type (about 18 500 in all). It checks for:
- text leaks;
- a distractor that equals the answer;
- known engines;
- the `stage2` tag;
- reading load: prompts of 45 words or fewer.

It also works out the answer again **from the diagram config the student sees**:
- column sums, ruler lengths and base-ten blocks;
- grid area (shoelace) and perimeter;
- clock, scale and jug readings;
- tally rows, shaded fractions, number-line points and area models.

`tools/stages.mjs` now expects four stages, with Stage 2 first and 20 topics.
