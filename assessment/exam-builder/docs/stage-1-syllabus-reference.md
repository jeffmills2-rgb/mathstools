# Stage 1: syllabus reference for the question banks

This is NESA Mathematics K–10 (2022), **Stage 1 (Years 1–2)**.

- The 16 outcome codes and statements below come from the NESA outcomes page (checked 2026-09-26).
- The content under each focus area is a **paraphrase for planning**. Check the A/B split against the official Stage 1 content document.
- In the 2022 syllabus, Stage 1 fractions sit in Geometric measure (MA1-GM-03: halves, quarters and eighths as parts of a whole length). This build gives fractions a topic of their own so they can be taught and assessed in sequence.
- Every prompt in the banks is original.

## Design principles for Stage 1 (read before adding a type)

These students are 6–8 years old and many are still learning to read, so the rules are stricter than for Stage 2.

1. **The picture IS the question.** Almost every type shows the maths as something to count, compare or complete:
   - ten-frames, dot and dice patterns, and pictures of objects;
   - base-ten blocks and hundred charts;
   - number lines and part–part–whole diagrams;
   - clocks and calendars;
   - balances, and rows of informal units.
2. **Very low reading load.**
   - Prompts are 1–2 short sentences of about 8 words each, with at most 25 words in all (the harness checks this).
   - Use common words only: "How many?", "Which is longer?", "Circle…", "Write the number."
   - Write numbers as numerals. Use no subordinate clauses.
   - A teacher can read any prompt aloud in under 10 seconds.
3. **Answers are short.** An answer is a number, a letter (A, B or C), or one word (more or less, longer or shorter, heavier or lighter, left or right). Multiple-choice options are short too.
4. **Big ideas.**
   - **Numbers:** a number can be made (partitioned) in different ways. Ten ones make one ten, and ten tens make one hundred. The position of a digit gives its value.
   - **Addition and subtraction:** part–part–whole. Addition and subtraction undo each other. Counting on from the bigger number and using doubles and bonds to 10 are efficient strategies.
   - **Equal groups:** skip counting counts equal groups. Sharing and grouping both make equal groups.
   - **Fractions:** a half means 2 EQUAL parts. Halving a half makes quarters, and halving again makes eighths.
   - **Measurement:** to measure, repeat the same unit with no gaps or overlaps. Bigger units give smaller counts.
   - **Time:** the hour hand shows the hour; the minute hand shows how far past the hour.
   - **Data:** a picture graph answers a question.
   - **Chance:** some things will happen, some might happen, and some won't happen.
5. **Skill building first, assessment-ready too.** Every type has an answer, working and marks. Types move from recognise, to represent, to reason.
6. **Engines.**
   - `manipulatives-engine` now also draws the Stage 1 models: `ten-frame`, `dice`, `objects` (picture icons, crossed-out items for take-away), `hundred-chart` (with blanks), `unit-length` (an object measured in cubes, paperclips or hands), and `calendar`.
   - It is reused for base-ten blocks, number lines, groups, arrays, shapes, fraction strips and shapes, the balance and tally charts.
   - Other engines reused:
     - `grid` for maps and area by covering;
     - `solids` for 3D objects;
     - `measure` for clocks;
     - `statistics` for picture graphs;
     - `probability` for spinners and the chance line;
     - `bar-model` for part–whole diagrams.

### Engine additions for Stage 1

**`manipulatives-engine`** gains these types:
- `objects`: picture collections. There are 12 icons. `crossed` crosses items out for take-away, `circle` rings a group, `items` gives a mixed row, `labels` names each position, `rowLabels` names each row, and an empty slot keeps rows lined up for comparing.
- `ten-frame`: one or two frames, in two colours.
- `dice`: dot patterns 1–6.
- `hundred-chart`: any window of the chart, with blank or shaded cells.
- `unit-length`: objects measured in cubes, paperclips, hands or rods, with the unit set per row.
- `calendar`.

Other engine changes:
- `objects` only puts a gap after 5 when a row holds 10.
- `probability-engine`: the chance scale keeps side room for long end words when `wordList` is used.

### Reading-load rules (enforced by `tools/stage1.mjs`)

- Prompts are 25 words or fewer. Prompts that list survey answers are exempt, because the list is the data.
- No word is longer than 13 letters.
- Answers are a number, a letter or a short phrase.

### Testing

`node tools/stage1.mjs` generates 80 questions per type (about 14 700 questions). It works out each answer again from the diagram config the child sees:
- picture counts and take-aways;
- ten-frames, dice and base-ten blocks;
- hundred-chart blanks and number-line points;
- informal-unit lengths;
- clocks (o'clock, half past, quarter past, quarter to);
- grid areas;
- tallies, picture graphs and fraction shapes.

`tools/stages.mjs` now expects five stages, with Stage 1 first and 20 topics.
